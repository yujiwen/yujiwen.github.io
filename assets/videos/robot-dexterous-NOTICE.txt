The portfolio's robot-dexterous and robot-precision MP4/JPEG files are rendered
derivatives. Changes include materials, lighting, camera and independently
authored finger/thumb/wrist animation. robot-precision also replaces forearm
and wrist visual geometry with authored curved casing and joint hardware;
the original hand meshes and joint hierarchy are retained.
